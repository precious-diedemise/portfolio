    03 / Influence

    What makes a stakeholder matter?

    Mitchell, Agle, and Wood's Stakeholder Salience Model (1997) considers three attributes that can shape the attention an organization gives a stakeholder.

    Power

    Ability to influence organizational decisions.

    Legitimacy

    A recognized or appropriate claim on the organization.

    Urgency

    A claim requiring timely attention.

    05 / Recommendations

    So, what would I recommend?

    Three practical priorities that follow from the analysis.

    01Give key stakeholders a voice.

    Build regular opportunities for member businesses, leadership, and major partners to provide input.

    02Approach prospective members differently.

    Use membership-focused outreach instead of treating prospective businesses like existing members.

    03Revisit the map.

    Reassess priorities as membership, involvement, partnerships, and organizational circumstances change.

    What this analysis cannot tell us: It is based on academic models, publicly available information, and reasoned assumptions rather than stakeholder interviews or direct validation. The recommendations are proposals, not verified practices or implemented results.
