02 / Stakeholder salience

Why do certain voices carry weight?

Mitchell, Agle, and Wood's model (1997) considers power, legitimacy, and urgency. Their combination helps explain stakeholder salience.

04 / Strategic recommendations

What would I recommend?

Three practical steps based on the stakeholder analysis.

01

Create consistent dialogue with key players.

Use member forums, regular leadership coordination, and partner check-ins to make engagement two-way.

02

Separate prospects from existing members.

Target nonmember entrepreneurs with membership information rather than treating them as current members.

03

Revisit the map as relationships change.

Check whether involvement, influence, and policy priorities still support the classifications.

Method & assumptions

Independent MBA application of stakeholder analysis frameworks to publicly available organizational context. Classifications were refined after professor feedback. No stakeholder interviews or commissioned research were conducted.

References & limitations

Cornelissen (2023), Corporate Communication; Mitchell, Agle & Wood (1997), Stakeholder Salience; stakeholder mapping associated with Mendelow (1981). Classifications are qualitative judgments, not observed Chamber outcomes.
