01 / Project overview

The opportunity was there. The budget wasn't.

The objective was straightforward: make a major professional development opportunity financially accessible to six students. The difficult part was bringing the costs, resources, people and logistics together.

Business challenge

Make attendance financially feasible.

Conference registration, accommodation and transportation created a substantial cost barrier. Registration pricing also changed during planning, requiring the budget and fundraising strategy to adapt.

My responsibility

Lead the plan, coordinate the team.

I initiated the project, built and revised the cost estimates, pursued funding, explored lower-cost alternatives and negotiated a driving arrangement. I worked with the NSA Vice President and travelers to coordinate execution.

02 / Financial planning

A budget that had to evolve.

The first estimate relied on discounted student tickets. When that registration category closed, I worked through new ticket costs, adjusted accommodation and explored a less expensive transportation option.

01

Registration became more expensive.

After $320 student tickets closed, the next available option was approximately $600 each.

Found an Insider route at $480 per person, avoiding $720 versus six $600 tickets.
02

Transportation had to be redesigned.

The original rental-and-gas estimate was $700. A UCM van offered a lower-cost approach.

Reduced the transportation budget to $300, a $400 estimated saving.
03

A cost saving created a new dependency.

Securing the van did not solve the question of who would drive it.

Negotiated with a professor who agreed to drive for the trip.
